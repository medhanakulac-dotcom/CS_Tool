04Playbook matrixsegment × product health · hidden · tap to show

Read across a row to see how the motion intensifies as health drops. Read down a column to see how the same health state gets a very different motion depending on segment value. A High + Unhealthy account gets a hands-on Rescue, while a Low + Unhealthy account gets an automated journey — same problem, proportional effort. Every account is reviewed weekly by the tool, so there is no per-segment schedule. The goal is always to move accounts left-to-right: Unhealthy → Adopted → Healthy.

Input 1 · Commercial segment

How valuable an account is to seatOS. Set commercially and reviewed each quarter.

High

Strategic, top-value accounts. Most hands-on attention, pushed toward full adoption.

Mid

Solid mid-value accounts. Lighter touch; 3 active features is an acceptable baseline.

Low

Smaller accounts served by automated motions: activation journeys, nudges, self-service.

Dormant

Inactive or at-risk, no live engagement. Reactive only.
